# 🏎️ APEX GRID

A 3D Formula Racing Game focused on speed, precision, AI competition, and immersive circuits.

## 🏁 About

APEX GRID is a 3D racing game where players can compete against AI drivers across multiple unique circuits or explore the tracks freely in Practice Mode.

The game focuses on realistic racing concepts such as racing lines, cornering, grip, lap times, and track limits.

## 🎮 Game Modes

### 🏆 Race Mode
Compete against a 4-car AI grid in a 3-lap race.

- AI opponents
- Live positions
- Lap timing
- Leaderboard
- Competitive racing

### 🛠️ Practice Mode

Explore the circuit freely without opponents or a lap limit.

Perfect for learning the track and improving your driving.

## 🏎️ Circuits

### Montalto Grand Prix
A high-speed circuit featuring long straights and two late-braking chicanes.

### Portofino Street Circuit
A tight and unforgiving street circuit where mistakes can quickly cost you time.

### Highland Flow Circuit
A fast-flowing circuit featuring sweeping corners and high-speed sections.

## 🎮 Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `W` / `↑` | Accelerate |
| `S` / `↓` | Brake / Reverse |
| `A` / `←` | Steer Left |
| `D` / `→` | Steer Right |
| `Space` | Boost |
| `Esc` | Menu / Pause |

## 🧠 Racing Mechanics

APEX GRID rewards clean and precise driving.

Going off the racing surface reduces grip and costs speed, making corner exits and racing lines important for achieving fast lap times.

Players need to balance:

- Speed
- Braking
- Cornering
- Grip
- Racing line
- Track limits
- Overtaking
